Theodore Rose

Hello! I am a senior at the University of Maryland, expecting to graduate in spring 2027 with a B.A. in Journalism and a B.S. in Information Science. I am currently the Data Editor at The Diamondback, a large independent student newspaper at the University of Maryland. My reporting, analysis and graphics work has been published by The Diamondback, the Baltimore Brew, Capital News Service and republished by The Baltimore Sun. When I am not working, I can be found buried in a good book, in a woodshop or cooking for my friends.
